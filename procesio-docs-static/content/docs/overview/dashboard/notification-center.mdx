The **Notification Center** is the bell icon in the topbar. A number on the bell shows how many notifications you have not read yet.

Click the bell to open the list. Each notification has a title, a message and the date and time it was sent. New ones are marked as unread.

PROCESIO notifies you, for example, when an export or an import of resources starts and when it completes (see [Import and export resources](/import-export-resources)), about workspace administration and resource usage, and when an action you use is being retired.

## Read and filter [#read-and-filter]

- Hover over an unread notification and click **Mark as read** to mark that one.
- Click **Mark all as read** to mark every notification as read.
- Switch on **Show only unread** to hide the ones you have read.

When you have no notifications, the list says *"No notifications yet"*. With **Show only unread** on and nothing new, it says *"No unread notifications"*.

![The notification list open from the bell, with unread notifications, the Show only unread toggle and Mark all as read](/images/docs/notifications/notification-center.png)

<Callout type="info">
Notifications that pop up on screen while you work, such as *"Custom URL was saved."*, are separate from this list. You can close them with their close button.
</Callout>
