You can sign in to PROCESIO with your email address and password, or with a Google, GitHub or Microsoft account.

![The PROCESIO Sign in page with the Google, GitHub and Microsoft buttons, the Email address and Password fields, Remember me, Forgot password? and the Sign in button](/images/docs/account/sign-in.png)

## Sign in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft [#single-sign-on]

1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Google**, **GitHub** or **Microsoft**.
2. A pop-up window opens. Sign in to that account and allow access.
3. The window closes and PROCESIO opens.

<Callout type="info">
The sign-in opens in a pop-up window. If nothing happens, your browser may be blocking pop-ups: allow pop-ups for PROCESIO and try again.
</Callout>

If the provider cannot be reached, PROCESIO shows *"Unexpected error encountered."* and says which provider is unavailable. Try again, or sign in with your email and password.

## Sign in with your email and password [#email-and-password]

1. On the **Sign in** page, under **or continue with email**, type your **Email address** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign in**.

If the email address or the password is wrong, you see *"Error! Invalid login credentials."*

Select **Remember me** to have PROCESIO sign you in automatically the next time you open it in this browser. Do not use it on a computer that other people use.

## Two-factor authentication [#two-factor]

If two-factor authentication (2FA) is on for your account, a **Code** field appears after you click **Sign in**. Type the code from your authenticator app and click **Sign in** again.

To turn on 2FA, or to learn what happens in a workspace that requires it, see [Two-factor authentication](/two-factor-authentication).

## Forgot your password [#forgot-password]

1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your **Email address** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email from PROCESIO and click the link. The link is valid for 24 hours.
4. On the **Change your password** page, type the new password in **New Password** and again in **Confirm Password**, then click **Change password**.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder.

Your new password must:

- be at least 8 characters long (and at most 256);
- contain at least one uppercase letter (A-Z);
- contain at least one lowercase letter (a-z);
- contain at least one digit (0-9);
- contain at least one special character, for example `?`, `!`, `#`, `%` or `$`.

## Too many failed attempts [#account-locked]

For your security, PROCESIO locks your account for a short time after several failed sign-in attempts. The **Sign in** page then says *"For security reasons, your account has been locked after several failed login attempts."*

Wait a little and try again. If you still cannot sign in, contact your administrator.

## Stay signed in and sign out [#sign-out]

PROCESIO keeps you signed in while you work. To sign out, open the user menu at the top right and click **Sign Out**.

## No account yet [#create-account]

On the **Sign in** page, click **Start for free** to create a PROCESIO account.

## Need help [#help]

If you cannot sign in, raise a ticket from the **Need help?** link on the **Sign in** page, or write to [support@procesio.com](mailto:support@procesio.com).
