An enterprise account has a **master workspace**. You use it to administer the account: create and manage **sub-workspaces**, manage users, and see the account's usage. Your teams build and run their processes in the sub-workspaces.

## Create a sub-workspace [#create]

1. Switch to the master workspace.
2. Click **Create workspace** on its **Dashboard**, or open **Workspaces** in the left menu and click **Create workspace** on the **Workspace Management** page.
3. In the **Create Workspace** panel, fill in the **General** tab (see below). You can also set the **Permissions** and **Data Retention** tabs now, or later.
4. Click **Create**.

The new sub-workspace is added to the list. Click the **Go to workspace** icon on its row to open it in a new tab.

![The Workspace Management page of a master workspace with the sub-workspace counter at its limit, the 2FA enforcement switch and the Create workspace button](/images/docs/workspaces/workspace-management.png)

## The sub-workspace limit [#limit]

A master workspace can hold a limited number of sub-workspaces. Next to the **Workspace Management** title you see how many you have and the limit, for example *"12 of 22 sub-workspaces"*. The counter changes colour as you get close to the limit. When you reach it, the counter reads, for example, *"22 of 22 - limit reached"*, and **Create workspace** is turned off, with a tooltip that says the master workspace has reached its limit. To create a new one, delete a sub-workspace you no longer need.

## Edit a sub-workspace [#edit]

On the **Workspace Management** page, click a sub-workspace's name or its **Edit** button. The **Update Workspace** panel has the same tabs as when you create one. Click **Save** to keep your changes.

### General [#general]

- **Workspace name**: required. The name follows the same rules as when you rename a workspace: at most 100 characters, no `/`, and unique among the sub-workspaces of the master workspace (letter case is ignored). See [Name rules](/rename-a-workspace#name-rules).
- **Process execution settings**: decides what happens when the account runs out of processing time.
  - *Suspend process execution on workspace when Company workspace resources reach soft limit to avoid extra cost generated.*
  - *Do not suspend process execution on workspace when Company workspace resources reach soft limit.* Processes keep running until the account's hard limit is reached, and the extra processing time is charged according to your plan.

  The **soft limit** is the processing time of your current plan. The **hard limit** is 30% more processing time on top of it. See [Processing time and usage](/processing-time-and-usage).

  If your account is licensed by threads instead, this setting is **Max threads**: the number of threads the sub-workspace may use.

<Callout type="info">
A new sub-workspace uses processing time from the resources of the company (master) workspace. Process execution is suspended when the company workspace reaches the hard limit.
</Callout>

### Permissions [#permissions]

Here you edit the sub-workspace's **Default Permissions**: the user type and permissions that new members get when they are invited to it. See [Users](/users) and [User Permissions and Roles Management](/user-permissions-and-roles-management).

### Data Retention [#data-retention]

Here you set the sub-workspace's **Process data retention**: whether the data of finished runs is deleted, and when. A process uses this setting when it has no data retention setting of its own. See [Data retention](/data-retention).

### Resource Tracking [#resource-tracking]

The **Record time analytics** switch decides whether time analytics are recorded for the sub-workspace. The change applies as soon as you switch it. The tab also shows the **Time analytics end date** and the **Time analytics storage limit**, or *Not set*.

<Callout type="info">
**Analytics is off by default for new sub-workspaces.** Turn on **Record time analytics** if you want to see the sub-workspace's processing time in [Analytics](/resource-tracking). While it is off, the analytics screens tell you that analytics is disabled for the workspace.
</Callout>

The **Resource Tracking** tab is shown when you edit an existing sub-workspace, to Owners, Co-Owners and Admins.

## Other actions on the Workspace Management page [#other-actions]

- **Copy** the ID of a sub-workspace with the copy icon next to its name.
- Click the number in the **USERS** column to see and invite the sub-workspace's users. See [Users](/users).
- **Delete** a sub-workspace with the delete icon. To confirm, type the workspace name exactly (the field is case-sensitive).

<Callout type="warn">
Deleting a sub-workspace deletes all the resources it contains. This can't be undone.
</Callout>

- **Enable 2FA Enforcement** makes two-factor authentication required in the account's workspaces. See [Two-factor authentication](/two-factor-authentication).
- Rename the master workspace with the pencil next to its name. See [Rename a workspace](/rename-a-workspace).
