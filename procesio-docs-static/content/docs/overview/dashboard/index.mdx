When you sign in to [PROCESIO](https://procesio.app/), you land on the **Dashboard** of your current workspace.

![The Dashboard of a shared workspace with the title and rename pencil, the Workspace ID, the Create process button and a form application tile](/images/docs/dashboard/dashboard.png)

## The header [#header]

- The title is *"&lt;workspace name&gt; Dashboard"*. In your personal workspace it greets you by name instead.
- Click the pencil next to the title to rename the workspace. See [Rename a workspace](/rename-a-workspace).
- **Workspace ID** shows the workspace's ID, with a copy icon. You need it, for example, to [run a process with an API key](/running-a-process-with-api-keys).
- **Create process** opens the Process Designer with a new process. In a master workspace the button is **Create workspace** instead. See [Create and configure a sub-workspace](/how-to-create-a-workspace-in-a-master-workspace).

## Form applications [#form-applications]

Forms that are marked as applications are shown as tiles at the top of the Dashboard, so you can open them in one click. See [Form applications](/form-applications).

## Plan and usage [#plan-and-usage]

The **Plan details** card (in a sub-workspace: **Workspace usage**) shows the usage of your plan for the period you choose. From here you can open **Your SaaS Agreement** and, outside sub-workspaces, **Buy Resources**. See [Resource Tracking](/resource-tracking) and [Processing time and usage](/processing-time-and-usage).

You see this card if you can view the workspace's details.

## Learn and get help [#learn]

- A carousel of short videos shows what you can do with PROCESIO.
- **Quick starters 📚** links to the FAQ, setting up your workspace, the Process Designer basics and demos, plus **Find more in Knowledgebase**, **Useful links on Linktree** and **Support**.
- **Help us spread the word! 🏆** links to the PROCESIO community on Discord and to review sites.

## Workspace switch [#workspace-switch]

Use the workspace switch in the upper-left corner to move to another workspace you have access to.

![](/images/docs/jKIRp8gmM3j_H5Bi9Arg5_switch1.png)

From the switch you can also open the **master workspace** of an enterprise account you administer.

![](/images/docs/SRQFWGO-pAzZJp5bstmcF_switch2.png)

## Navigation [#navigation]

The left menu leads to every section of PROCESIO. The section you are in is highlighted. Less-used tools, such as **Analytics** and the **Resources Map**, are under **More tools**.

![](/images/docs/PhEeMOPUl9DzFDIcr6rnD_tdy.png)

## The topbar [#topbar]

In the upper-right corner you find:

- **Appearance**: light, dark or system theme, and high contrast. See [Appearance and accessibility](/appearance-and-accessibility).
- The **Notification Center** (the bell). See [Notification center](/notification-center).
- Your avatar, which opens the user menu: your name, **Preferences** (keyboard shortcuts and two-factor authentication, with **2FA enabled** once it is on) and **Sign Out**. See [Your profile and user menu](/user-profile).
