Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from an authenticator app each time you sign in, on top of your password.

## Turn on 2FA for your account [#turn-on]

1. Open the user menu at the top right of PROCESIO.
2. Under **Preferences**, click **Enable 2FA**.
3. A setup window opens. Follow its steps to connect your authenticator app.

When setup finishes you see *"Two-factor authentication enabled!"*, and the user menu shows **2FA enabled** instead of **Enable 2FA**.

<Callout type="info">
The setup opens in a pop-up window. If your browser blocks it, you see *"The setup window was blocked. Please, allow pop-ups for this site and try again."* Allow pop-ups for PROCESIO and click **Enable 2FA** again.
</Callout>

If 2FA is already on for your account, the menu shows **2FA enabled** and there is nothing to set up.

## Require 2FA in a master workspace [#require]

The owner and co-owners of a master workspace can require 2FA for everyone who works in it.

1. Open the master workspace and click **Workspaces** in the left menu.
2. At the top of the **Workspace Management** page, switch on **Enable 2FA Enforcement**.

![The Workspace Management header with the Enable 2FA Enforcement switch](/images/docs/two-factor/workspace-list-2fa.png)

The setting applies to the master workspace **and to every workspace inside it**, including workspaces created after you switched it on. It doesn't affect anyone's personal workspace.

<Callout type="info">
Turn on 2FA for your own account first. Once enforcement is on, anyone without 2FA, including you, has to set it up before they can keep working in these workspaces.
</Callout>

## When a workspace requires 2FA [#required-dialog]

If you open a workspace that requires 2FA and your account doesn't have it yet, PROCESIO shows this message:

![The "Two-factor authentication required" dialog with the buttons Switch to personal workspace and Enable 2FA](/images/docs/two-factor/require-2fa.png)

* **Enable 2FA** opens the setup window. As soon as 2FA is on, the workspace opens.
* **Switch to personal workspace** takes you to your personal workspace instead.
