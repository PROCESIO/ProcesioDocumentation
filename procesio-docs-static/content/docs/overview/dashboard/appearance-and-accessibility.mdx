The **Appearance** menu lets you choose how PROCESIO looks: a light or dark theme, and a high contrast mode with stronger borders and lines that are easier to see.

## Open the Appearance menu [#open]

Click the **Appearance** icon at the top right of the top bar, to the left of the notifications. The icon is a sun in the light theme and a moon in the dark theme. You also find it on the **Sign in** page, in the Form Designer and in the Action Designer.

## Choose a theme [#theme]

In the **Appearance** menu, click one of:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Always uses the light theme. |
| **Dark** | Always uses the dark theme. |
| **System** | Follows your computer's setting, and changes when your computer switches between light and dark. This is the default. |

A check mark shows the theme in use.

## Turn high contrast on or off [#high-contrast]

High contrast makes borders and lines stronger and easier to see.

- To turn it on, open the **Appearance** menu and click **Turn on high contrast**.
- To turn it off, click **Turn off high contrast**.

High contrast works with both the light and the dark theme.

## Automatic high contrast [#automatic]

If your computer is set to use more contrast, PROCESIO turns on high contrast automatically. If you then click **Turn off high contrast**, PROCESIO keeps it off, even though your computer asks for more contrast.

## Where your choice is kept [#saved]

PROCESIO remembers your theme and contrast choices in the browser you use. On another browser or computer, choose them again.
