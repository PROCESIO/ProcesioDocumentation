**Shapes** are rectangles, ellipses and diamonds you can drop on the canvas to
group related actions, mark a stage of the flow, or write a note next to it. They
are there for whoever reads the process — including you in six months.

<Callout type="info">
  **A shape never runs.** It is not an action: it has no ports, it cannot be
  connected, it does not appear in a run, it consumes nothing, and it never affects
  validation. It is drawing on the canvas, and it is saved with the process.
</Callout>

## Add a shape [#add-a-shape]

1. In the toolbar, switch from **Actions** to **Shapes**.
2. Drag the shape you want onto the canvas, or double-click it to drop one in the
   middle.

There are three:

| Shape         | Typical use                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Rectangle** | Boxing a group of actions that belong together.         |
| **Ellipse**   | Calling out a start, an end, or a note.                 |
| **Diamond**   | Marking where a decision happens, next to a Decisional. |

Drag a shape by its body to move it, and use the handles on its edge to resize it.

## Write on a shape [#write-on-a-shape]

**Double-click the shape** and type. Press **Enter** to save what you wrote.

A shape holds two separate pieces of text:

- A **title**, which sits at the top of the shape and can be aligned left, centre or
  right.
- The **body text**, in the middle, which is what you get by double-clicking.

## Style a shape [#style-a-shape]

Click a shape to open its panel:

| Setting             | What it does                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Fill color**      | Pick one of the preset swatches, or open the picker for any colour you like.                       |
| **Fill opacity**    | 0–100%. Lower it so the actions underneath stay readable through the shape.                        |
| **Title**           | The shape's heading.                                                                               |
| **Title alignment** | Left, centre or right.                                                                             |
| **Order**           | **Bring to front** / **Send to back** — which shape wins where two overlap.                        |

<Callout type="info">
  **Use a low fill opacity for a shape that sits behind actions.** A shape drawn at
  full opacity over a group of actions hides them until you send it to the back.
</Callout>

## The right-click menu [#the-right-click-menu]

Right-click a shape for:

- **Bring to front**
- **Send to back**
- **Delete**

## Shapes and actions behave differently [#shapes-and-actions]

A few differences worth knowing, because they surprise people:

- **Copy and paste does not apply to shapes.** Ctrl + C / Ctrl + V copy *actions*.
  To get a second shape, drag another one from the toolbar.
- **Shapes do join a multi-selection**, so Ctrl + click picks up shapes and actions
  together and drags them as one group.
- **A shape is never validated**, so it can never be the reason a process is invalid.
