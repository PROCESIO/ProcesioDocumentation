A chain follows one request from its first submission to its last step. It groups everything that belongs to the same piece of work:

- **Every submission starts a chain.** A simple form that is submitted once is a chain with one step.
- **Each step of a task form is added to the chain.** When a form has assignees or approvers, every submission of a step (for example the requester's part, then the manager's approval) adds a step to the same chain.
- **A form opened from another form joins its chain.** When a form's **Trigger form** event opens a second form, the second form's submission joins the first form's chain. This happens when the person filling it is signed in and works in the form's workspace. Otherwise the second form's submission is saved on its own.

## See the chains of a form [#see-the-chains-of-a-form]

1. Go to **Forms & Tasks** > **Form Designer** and open **Submissions** for the form.
2. Switch from **Submissions** to **Chains** at the top of the table. The reload icon next to **Chains** refreshes the list.

The Chains list has one row per chain, with these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **STATUS** | The state of the chain's latest step: *in progress*, *completed*, *approved* or *rejected*. A chain is *in progress* while a step is still waiting for someone. |
| **START DATE** | When the chain started. |
| **END DATE** | When the chain finished. |
| **SUBMITTED BY** | The people who submitted its steps. |
| Your additional columns | The values of the form fields you chose in **Flow settings**. See below. |

## Open one chain [#open-one-chain]

Click **Show details** on a row. The chain page shows a summary at the top (**Start date**, **End date**, **Status**, **Submitted by**) and one row per step, with the form and step name, its status, dates and the person who submitted it. Click **Show step details** to see what was submitted in that step. You can reload this page; it keeps its own address.

## Add columns to the Chains list (Flow settings) [#flow-settings]

By default, the Chains list shows **STATUS**, **START DATE** and **END DATE**. To show the value of a form field as well:

1. Open the form in the Form Designer.
2. In the side tabs on the right, click **Flow settings** (the workflow icon).
3. Click **+ Add additional column**.
4. In **Element**, pick the form element whose value you want to see. The list shows the elements of this form.
5. **Column header** is filled in from the element's label. Change it if you want a different title. It is required.
6. Add more columns the same way. Drag a column to change the order, or click the delete icon to remove it.
7. Click **Save** in the panel, then save **and** publish the form.

![The Flow settings panel with two additional columns, each with an Element and a Column header, and the + Add additional column button](/images/docs/forms/form-flow-settings.png)

<Callout type="info">
Set the columns on each form that takes part in your chains, not only on the first one.
</Callout>
