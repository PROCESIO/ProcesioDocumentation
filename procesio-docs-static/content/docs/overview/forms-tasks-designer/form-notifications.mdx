Form notifications send an e-mail to the addresses you choose when someone submits the form. You can be told about successful submissions, failed submissions, or both.

## Turn on notifications [#turn-on-notifications]

1. Open the form in the Form Designer.
2. In the side tabs on the right, click **Form notifications** (the envelope icon). The **Set form notifications** panel opens.
3. Turn on **Set email notifications**.
4. In **Email addresses**, type the addresses that should get the e-mail. Separate several addresses with `;`, for example `anna@example.com;team@example.com`.
5. Under **Notification type**, choose when to send the e-mail. Both are ticked by default:
   - **Notify when the form is submitted successfully**
   - **Notify when the form submission fails**

   Keep at least one ticked. With neither ticked, the panel shows *"You need to select a type from above in order to complete the notification configuration."*
6. Click **Save** in the panel.
7. Save the form, then publish it. The notification settings are part of the form, so the published form uses them only after you publish it again.

{/* SCREENSHOT: form-notifications-panel.png — the Set form notifications panel with Set email notifications on, two addresses separated by ; and both notification types ticked */}

## Turn off notifications [#turn-off-notifications]

Open **Form notifications**, turn off **Set email notifications**, click **Save**, then save and publish the form.

## Who can change them [#who-can-change-them]

You see the **Form notifications** tab if you can view forms in the workspace. To change the settings you need permission to edit forms; otherwise the panel is read-only.
