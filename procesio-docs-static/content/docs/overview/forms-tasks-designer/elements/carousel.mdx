description: "The Carousel element shows a set of slides one at a time, with arrows, slide indicators, swipe and optional autoplay to move between them."
---

The Carousel element is a container that shows its content as slides, one slide at a time. Each slide holds its own form elements: text, images, fields, buttons. The person filling the form moves between slides with arrows, slide indicators, the keyboard or a swipe, or the carousel moves on its own.

All the slides belong to the same form. Values entered on any slide are submitted with the form, whichever slide is showing when the person clicks submit.

## Key use cases [#key-use-cases]

- An introduction or onboarding tour shown at the top of a form
- A gallery of images or product cards, one at a time
- A long form split into short parts without the strict order of a [Stepper](/stepper)
- Announcements or tips that rotate on their own with autoplay

## Add the element to a form [#add-the-element]

1. Open the form in the Form Designer.
2. In the **Toolbar**, open the **Structure** tab (or type `Carousel` in **Search element**).
3. Drag **Carousel** onto the form.
4. Select the carousel to open its settings.

![The Carousel element in the Structure tab of the Toolbar: A container for slides shown one at a time](/images/docs/forms/carousel-toolbar.png)

A new carousel has three slides. Its arrows and slide indicators work in the designer too, so you can move to any slide and fill it.

## Add elements into the slides [#slides]

Move the carousel to the slide you want to fill, then drag elements from the **Toolbar** onto it. An empty slide shows *Drag and drop slide elements here*. A slide can hold any number of elements, including containers such as a [Section](/section) or [Columns](/columns).

The **Slides** setting lists the carousel's slides in order:

- **Add slide** adds a new empty slide at the end.
- The text box of a slide renames it. The name is used to refer to the slide in events and scripts.
- The gear icon opens the slide's own settings: **Visible**, **Name** and **ID**, and on the **Styling** tab the layout of the elements inside the slide.
- The trash icon deletes the slide.

When you select an element on the canvas that sits on another slide, the carousel moves to that slide.

<Callout type="info">
A carousel cannot be placed inside another carousel. The designer does not let you drop a carousel, or a container that holds a carousel, onto a slide.
</Callout>

### Which slides the person filling the form sees [#visible-slides]

On the form, a slide is shown only when it is visible and holds at least one visible element. Hidden slides and empty slides are skipped by the arrows, the indicators and autoplay, and are not counted (a carousel with one hidden slide out of three shows 2 indicators). If you hide a slide before the one the person is on, they stay on the same slide.

When there is only one slide to show, the carousel shows it without arrows or indicators.

## Settings [#settings]

![The settings of a Carousel element: the Slides list with three slides and Add slide, Show arrows on, Arrows position Bottom by the indicators, Show slide indicators on, Indicator style Dots, Controls alignment Center, Loop off, Swipe and mouse drag on, Autoplay off, Autoplay interval 5, Transition effect Slide, Transition duration 450, Visible, Name, ID and the onSlideChange event](/images/docs/forms/carousel-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Slides** | The carousel's slides, in order. See [Add elements into the slides](#slides). |
| **Show arrows** | On (default): previous and next arrows are shown. |
| **Arrows position** | Where the arrows sit: **Bottom, by the indicators** (default), **Sides of the slide**, or **Over the slide**. At the bottom the arrows are plain icons; on the sides and over the slide they are round buttons. |
| **Show slide indicators** | On (default): one indicator per slide is shown under the slides. Clicking an indicator moves to that slide. |
| **Indicator style** | **Dots** (default), **Lines**, or **Counter (2 / 4)**, which shows the current slide number and the number of slides as text instead of clickable indicators. |
| **Controls alignment** | Where the row of indicators and controls sits: **Left**, **Center** (default) or **Right**. |
| **Loop** | Off (default): the previous arrow stops at the first slide and the next arrow stops at the last. On: the next arrow goes from the last slide back to the first, and the previous arrow from the first to the last. |
| **Swipe and mouse drag** | On (default): the person can move between slides by swiping on a touch screen or dragging with the mouse. See [Swipe and drag](#swipe). |
| **Autoplay** | Off (default). On: the carousel moves to the next slide on its own. See [Autoplay](#autoplay). |
| **Autoplay interval (s)** | How many seconds each slide is shown when **Autoplay** is on. Between `1` and `60`, default `5`. |
| **Transition effect** | The animation between two slides: **Slide** (default), **Fade**, **Zoom**, **Cover**, **Flip** or **None**. **Slide**, **Cover** and **Flip** follow the direction of the move: forward animates one way, back the other. |
| **Transition duration (ms)** | How long the animation lasts, in milliseconds. Between `0` and `3000`, default `450`. `0` changes the slide at once. |
| **Visible** | Show or hide the whole carousel. |
| **Name**, **ID** | The element's name and ID, used to refer to the carousel in events and scripts. |

The two number fields keep their values within their limits. Under each, the field shows its range (*Between 1 and 60.*, *Between 0 and 3000.*). A value outside the range is changed to the nearest limit when you leave the field, and that is the value saved with the form. Leave a field empty to use its default.

<Callout type="info">
If both **Show arrows** and **Show slide indicators** are off, the carousel still keeps a way to the other slides: the arrows appear when the person reaches the carousel with the Tab key.
</Callout>

## What the person filling the form sees [#runtime]

After clicking the next arrow, the second slide is shown and the second dot is filled:

![The same carousel on its second slide, Meet your team, with the second of the three dots filled](/images/docs/forms/carousel-preview-slide-2.png)

### Keyboard [#keyboard]

- The arrows and the indicators are buttons, so the person can reach them with Tab and use them with Enter or Space.
- While an arrow or an indicator has focus, the Left and Right arrow keys move to the previous or next slide.
- Screen readers announce the carousel, the position of each slide (for example *2 of 3*) and every move.

### Swipe and drag [#swipe]

With **Swipe and mouse drag** on and more than one slide, the person can drag a slide sideways with a finger or the mouse. A short drag springs back; a longer one moves to the next or previous slide. Dragging up or down scrolls the page as usual, and a drag that starts on a field, a button or a link is left to that element, so typing and clicking inside a slide work as normal.

Swipe works on the form only, not while you edit the carousel in the designer.

### Autoplay [#autoplay]

With **Autoplay** on, the carousel moves to the next slide every **Autoplay interval (s)** seconds.

- It pauses while the pointer is over the carousel and goes on when the pointer leaves.
- It stops once the person moves into the carousel with the keyboard or clicks a control in it.
- A pause button is shown with the indicators. The person can stop the slideshow with it and start it again with the same button.
- For people whose device asks for reduced motion, the carousel does not play on its own and no pause button is shown. They move between slides with the arrows and indicators.

Autoplay does not run while you edit the form in the designer.

### Required fields on other slides [#required-fields]

A required field can sit on a slide that is not showing. When the person submits the form with such a field empty, the form is not submitted, and the indicator of that slide shows a warning icon (screen readers read it as *has a field to fix*). Clicking the indicator moves to the slide, where the field is marked. The warning goes away as soon as the field is filled.

With **Indicator style** set to **Counter (2 / 4)** there are no indicators to carry the warning, so consider **Dots** or **Lines** for a carousel with required fields.

### A carousel in a List [#in-a-list]

A carousel can be placed inside a [List](/list). Each row of the list has its own carousel, and moving one row's carousel does not move the others.

## Events [#events]

The carousel has one event, under **Events** in its settings:

| Event | When it fires |
| --- | --- |
| **onSlideChange** | Each time the carousel moves to another slide. The `$event` variable holds the index of the slide now shown, starting from `0`. |

For example, use **onSlideChange** to show a field or update a value when the person reaches the last slide.

## Move between slides from JavaScript [#javascript]

Two functions move a carousel one slide forward or back, the same as clicking its arrows. Call them from any **Trigger JAVASCRIPT** event in the form, for example the **onClick** event of a button:

| Function | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **goToNextSlide()** | Moves to the next slide. On the last slide it does nothing, unless **Loop** is on: then it goes back to the first slide. |
| **goToPreviousSlide()** | Moves to the previous slide. On the first slide it does nothing, unless **Loop** is on: then it goes to the last slide. |

```javascript
ProcesioForm.data.fields.Carousel1.goToNextSlide();
ProcesioForm.data.fields.Carousel1.goToPreviousSlide();
```

Here `Carousel1` is the name of your Carousel element. Moving the carousel this way also fires its **onSlideChange** event.

- A carousel inside a List does not offer these functions.
- The designer's **Preview** doesn't run form scripts. Test on the published form.

## Styling [#styling]

On the **Styling** tab of the carousel's settings you can change how it looks. Turn on the check box next to a property to override the form theme for this carousel.

| Property | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gap** | `16px` | The space between the slides and the controls. |
| **Padding vertical**, **Padding horizontal** | `16px` | The space between the edge of a slide and its content. |
| **Surface background color** | Neutral 50 | The background of the slides. |
| **Surface border radius**, **Surface border width**, **Surface border color** | `4px`, `1px`, Border | The border around the slides. |
| **Slide min height** | `0px` | The minimum height of a slide. Set it to keep the carousel the same height on short and tall slides. |
| **Arrow color** | Neutral 700 | The colour of the plain arrows (at the bottom), the pause button and the counter. |
| **Round arrow background color**, **Round arrow icon color** | Neutral 700, Neutral 0 | The colours of the round arrows (on the sides or over the slide). |
| **Arrow border radius** | `50%` | The shape of the round arrows. |
| **Indicator color**, **Indicator fill**, **Indicator color active** | Neutral 800, Neutral 100, Neutral 800 | The colours of the dots and lines, and of the current one. |

**CSS Classes** applies classes from the form's own CSS code (**Form settings** → **Switch to code**).

Each slide has its own **Styling** tab too (open it with the gear icon in **Slides**): **Gap** (default `10px`) between the elements in the slide, and **Inner direction**, **Inner main axis** and **Inner cross axis** to lay them out in a column or a row.

You can also style every carousel of the form at once in the form's **Theme**.
