**Subscription and billing** shows the subscriptions of your account, their billing dates and prices, and how much of your plan you have used.

## Open the page [#open]

In the left menu, click **Subscription and billing**.

![The Subscription and billing page: the Subscription details table with the Buy resources button, and Plan usage below it](/images/docs/subscription/subscription-and-billing.png)

The page is available in a master workspace, to users who can manage the master workspace. You do not see it in sub-workspaces or in your personal workspace.

## Subscription details [#subscription-details]

The **Subscription details** table has one row per subscription, with these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | The name of the plan. |
| **Processing time** | The monthly processing time included, in hours. |
| **Started on** | When the subscription started. |
| **Ends on** | When the current period ends. |
| **Next billing cycle** | The day of the next billing. It shows `-` once the subscription has expired. |
| **Standard price (EUR)** | The price without VAT. |
| **Paid price** | The price you paid, with VAT, in the currency you paid in. |

Hover over the info icon next to a price column to read how it is calculated. The price you pay may vary with the daily currency exchange rate.

## Turn auto-renew on or off [#auto-renew]

Each subscription has an **Auto-renew** checkbox. Check it to renew the subscription automatically at the end of the period, or clear it to stop the renewal. A message confirms *"Subscription auto-renew was updated successfully."*

You cannot change auto-renew on a subscription that has already expired.

## Request a refund [#refund]

You can ask for a refund within 30 days of buying a subscription.

1. In the subscription's row, click **Refund**.
2. In the **Refund payment?** dialog, pick a reason from the list.
3. Click **Refund**.

The request is reviewed before it is approved, and you receive emails about its status. The row then shows **Refunded**.

**Refund** is disabled when a refund is not possible: the tooltip says *"Refund is not possible"*, or *"Refund is possible only within 30 days from acquisition."* once the 30 days have passed.

## Buy more processing time [#buy-resources]

Click **Buy Resources** at the top of the page. In the dialog, under **Select monthly processing time:**, pick the number of hours per month you want. Each option shows its monthly price.

## Plan usage [#plan-usage]

Under the table, **Plan usage** shows your **Plan**, your **Monthly processing time** and how much processing time you have used. See [Processing time and usage](/processing-time-and-usage) for how to read these figures.

Click **My billing and payment** to open your billing and payment account with our payment provider in a new tab.
