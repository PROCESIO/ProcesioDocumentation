The **Resources Map** draws the connections between the processes, webhooks and schedules of a workspace. Use it to see what starts a process, which subprocesses it calls, and what else would be affected if you change it.

## Open the Resources Map [#open]

In the left menu, open **More tools** and click **Resources Map**.

The Resources Map is available in your personal workspace and in sub-workspaces. You see it only if you can view processes, webhooks and schedules in the workspace.

## Show the connections of one resource [#show-connections]

1. In **Show connections for:**, open the list and pick a process, webhook or schedule. Use the **All**, **Processes**, **Webhooks** and **Schedules** tabs or **Search by name** to find it.
2. The map shows the resource and everything connected to it.

The **Legend** shows which icon stands for a **Process**, a **Webhook** and a **Schedule**.

A connection is drawn:

- from a webhook or a schedule to the process it starts;
- from a process to each subprocess it starts with [Call Subprocess](/how-to/call-subprocess) or [Trigger Subprocess](/how-to/trigger-subprocess), and on to their own subprocesses.

If the resource has no connections, the map says *"The component does not have connections"*.

Click an item on the map to see its details in a read-only panel: the settings of a webhook or a schedule, or the variables, webhooks, schedules and credentials of a process. Double-click a process to open it in the Process Designer.
