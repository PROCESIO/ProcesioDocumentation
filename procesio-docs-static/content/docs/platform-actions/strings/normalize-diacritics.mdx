**Normalize Diacritics** removes accents and other diacritical marks from letters: `é` becomes `e`, `ă` becomes `a`, `ș` becomes `s`. Use it before comparing names typed with and without diacritics, or before sending text to a system that accepts only plain Latin letters (file names, IDs, old databases).

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **String** and pick **Normalize Diacritics** (or type *Normalize Diacritics* in the search box).

![The Normalize Diacritics action properties: Input Text and Output Text](/images/docs/actions/string-normalize-diacritics/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Input Text** | Input | Text | Yes | The text to clean. |
| **Output Text** | Output | Text | Yes | The same text without diacritical marks. |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- Upper and lower case are kept.
- **Only marks added on top of or under a letter are removed.** Letters that are separate letters in their alphabet, such as `ø`, `ł`, `ß` or `æ`, are not changed (third example: `ó` becomes `o`, but `Ł` stays). Use [Replace](/how-to/replace) for those.
- Spaces, digits and punctuation are not changed.
