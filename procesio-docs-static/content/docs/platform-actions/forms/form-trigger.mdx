title: "Form Trigger"
icon: "📄"
description: "Creates a new form instance (a task) from one of your form templates, so that the form's assignees can fill it in."
---

**Form Trigger** creates a new instance of a form from a form template you built in the [Forms & Tasks Designer](/forms-tasks-designer). It is how a process hands work to people: the new instance goes to the form's first [task assignee](/task-assignees-and-approvals), who finds it under [Assigned to me](/assigned-to-me). Use it, for example, to open an approval task when a process receives a new invoice, or to send an onboarding checklist when a new employee is added.

<Callout type="info">
Form Trigger works in the opposite direction from form events. A **form event** starts a process when someone submits a form (see [Trigger events for form and elements](/trigger-events-for-form-and-elements)). **Form Trigger** is a process step that *creates* a form instance.
</Callout>

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **Forms** and pick **Form Trigger** (or type *Form Trigger* in the search box).

![The Form Trigger action properties: Form Template, Result and Form Id](/images/docs/actions/forms-form-trigger/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Form Template** (**Select Form Template** in newer versions) | Input | Form | Yes | The form to create an instance of. Pick one of the forms of your workspace. |
| **Result** (**Form successfully triggered** in newer versions) | Output | Boolean (true/false) | No | `true` if the form instance was created, `false` if not. |
| **Form Id** | Output | Text (ID) | No | The ID of the new form instance, or empty if it was not created. |

## How the new instance is set up [#how-the-new-instance-is-set-up]
- The instance starts from the form as it is defined in the template: its name, its elements and their settings.
- If the form has task assignees, the instance is placed on the **first assignee step** (the section, stepper step or tab with the lowest order). Those assignees see it in **Assigned to me** and, as described in [Task Assignees and Approvals](/task-assignees-and-approvals), are notified by email.
- The instance is created on behalf of the user who runs the process.
- Once created, the instance behaves like any other form task: it appears in the form's [submissions](/submissions) and its events run when assignees submit it.

## Example [#example]
A process receives an invoice from a webhook, saves it, then uses **Form Trigger** with **Form Template** *Invoice approval*. The form's first step is assigned to the finance team, so a new *Invoice approval* task appears under **Assigned to me** for them. **Result** is `true` and **Form Id** holds the new instance's ID; a [Decisional](/how-to/decisional) on **Result** sends an alert email when it is `false`.

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- **A failed creation does not fail the step.** When the form instance cannot be created, the step still succeeds, with **Result** `false` and **Form Id** empty. Check **Result** if the rest of your process depends on the task.
- **The step fails** if no form template is selected (*"Form template is null"*) or the selected form cannot be read.
- The form must belong to the same workspace as the process.
- **Test Action creates a real form instance.** Testing the action is not a dry run: the instance and its task are created and assignees may be notified.
- Form Trigger does not pre-fill the form's fields with process data.
