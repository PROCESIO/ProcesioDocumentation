title: "Get Day Of Week"
icon: "📕"
description: "Returns the position of a date in its week, as a number, with the numbering you choose."
---

**Get Day Of Week** returns which day of the week a date is, as a number. You choose whether the week starts on Sunday or on Monday, and whether counting starts at 0 or 1. Use it to schedule work for a given weekday, or to calculate how many days are left until the end of the week.

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Get Day Of Week** (or type *Get Day Of Week* in the search box).

![The Get Day Of Week action properties: Date, Numbering and Day Number](/images/docs/actions/datetime-get-day-of-week/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Date** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The date to check. |
| **Numbering** | Input | Option list | Yes | How days are numbered. See the table below. |
| **Day Number** | Output | Integer | Yes | The number of the day in its week. |

| Numbering | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week begins on Sunday (1) and ends on Saturday (7) | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 1 |
| Week begins on Monday (1) and ends on Sunday (7) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Week begins on Monday (0) and ends on Sunday (6) | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

## Example [#example]
| Date | Numbering | Day Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `2026-11-08` (Sunday) | Week begins on Sunday (1)… | `1` |
| `2026-11-08` (Sunday) | Week begins on Monday (1)… | `7` |
| `2026-11-08` (Sunday) | Week begins on Monday (0)… | `6` |
| `2026-11-09` (Monday) | Week begins on Sunday (1)… | `2` |
| `2026-11-09` (Monday) | Week begins on Monday (1)… | `1` |
| `2026-11-09` (Monday) | Week begins on Monday (0)… | `0` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- You get a number, not a name. For the name (`Monday`, `luni`, `Montag`…), use [Format DateTime](/how-to/format-datetime) with the format `dddd` and the language you need.
- A date with a UTC offset is first converted to UTC. An empty **Date** stops the step with an error.
