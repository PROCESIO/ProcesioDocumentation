**Today** gives you the current date, with the time set to `00:00:00`. Use it to stamp a record with the day it was processed, or as the starting point for date calculations such as "30 days from today".

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Today** (or type *Today* in the search box).

![The Today action properties: the Current Date output](/images/docs/actions/datetime-today/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Current Date** | Output | Date and time | Yes | Map a Date and time variable here. It receives today's date at midnight. |

The action has no inputs.

## Example [#example]
Run on 10 November 2026 → **Current Date** `2026-11-10T00:00:00+00:00`.

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- The date is the **UTC** date of the PROCESIO server, not your local date. Between midnight and your UTC offset (for example 00:00–02:00 in Bucharest in winter) you still get yesterday's date. If you need the date in a given time zone, take [UTC Now](/how-to/utc-now) and convert it with [Timezone Converter](/how-to/timezone-converter), then read the parts you need.
- The time is always `00:00:00`. For the current time, use [UTC Now](/how-to/utc-now).
