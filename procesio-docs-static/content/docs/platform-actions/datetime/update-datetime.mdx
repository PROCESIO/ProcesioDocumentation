**Update DateTime** moves a date forward or back by a whole number of years, months, days, hours, minutes or seconds. Use it to calculate due dates ("invoice date + 30 days"), reminders ("start time − 15 minutes") or expiry dates.

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Update DateTime** (or type *Update DateTime* in the search box).

![The Update DateTime action properties: Date, Unit Of Time, Change Amount and Updated Date](/images/docs/actions/datetime-update-datetime/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Date** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The date to start from. |
| **Unit Of Time** | Input | Option list | Yes | **Year**, **Month**, **Day**, **Hours**, **Minutes** or **Seconds**. |
| **Change Amount** | Input | Integer | Yes | How many units to add. Use a negative number to go back in time. Default `0`. |
| **Updated Date** | Output | Date and time | Yes | The new date. |

## Example [#example]
| Date | Unit Of Time | Change Amount | Updated Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2024-03-01T10:00:00` | Day | `-1` | `2024-02-29T10:00:00` |
| `2024-01-31T10:00:00` | Month | `1` | `2024-02-29T10:00:00` |
| `2024-02-29T10:00:00` | Year | `1` | `2025-02-28T10:00:00` |
| `2024-12-31T22:00:00` | Hours | `5` | `2025-01-01T03:00:00` |
| `2024-12-31T22:00:00` | Minutes | `-90` | `2024-12-31T20:30:00` |
| `2024-12-31T23:59:30` | Seconds | `45` | `2025-01-01T00:00:15` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- **Month ends and leap years:** when the target month is shorter, the day moves to the last day of that month (31 January + 1 month = 29 February in a leap year; 29 February + 1 year = 28 February). Adding years, months or days never changes the time of day.
- **Whole numbers only:** **Change Amount** must be an integer. `1.5` stops the step with an error. To add a day and a half, add `36` hours instead.
- **Time zone:** a date with a UTC offset is first converted to UTC, and the result has no offset: `2024-12-31T23:00:00+02:00` + 1 day gives `2025-01-01T21:00:00`.
- Days are calendar days. To skip weekends, check each new date with [Is Weekday](/how-to/is-weekday).
