title: "Date Difference"

description: "Counts the days, weeks, months, quarters or years between two dates."

**Date Difference** tells you how far apart two dates are, in days, weeks, months, quarters or years. Use it to check how old an invoice is, how many months a contract has run, or whether a deadline has passed. For hours, minutes and seconds, use [Time Difference](/how-to/time-difference).

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Date Difference** (or type *Date Difference* in the search box).

![The Date Difference action properties: Unit of Time, First Datetime, Second Datetime and Difference](/images/docs/actions/datetime-date-difference/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unit of Time** | Input | Option list | Yes | **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**. |
| **First Datetime** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The start date. |
| **Second Datetime** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The end date. |
| **Difference** | Output | Integer | Yes | **Second Datetime** minus **First Datetime**, in the unit you chose. Negative when the second date is earlier. |

## How each unit is counted [#how-each-unit-is-counted]
- **Day**: full 24-hour periods between the two moments. A remainder of less than a day is dropped, so the time of day matters.
- **Week**: the number of week boundaries crossed, with weeks running Sunday to Saturday. Saturday to the next day, Sunday, already counts as `1`.
- **Month**, **Quarter**, **Year**: the number of calendar months, quarters or years crossed. The day of the month is ignored, so 31 January to 1 February is `1` month.

## Example [#example]
| Unit of Time | First Datetime | Second Datetime | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day | `2024-01-01T00:00:00` | `2024-03-01T00:00:00` | `60` |
| Day | `2024-03-01T00:00:00` | `2024-01-01T00:00:00` | `-60` |
| Day | `2024-01-01T12:00:00` | `2024-01-02T11:00:00` | `0` (only 23 hours) |
| Week | `2026-11-07` (Saturday) | `2026-11-08` (Sunday) | `1` |
| Week | `2026-11-02` (Monday) | `2026-11-15` (Sunday) | `2` |
| Month | `2024-01-31` | `2024-02-01` | `1` |
| Quarter | `2024-03-31` | `2024-04-01` | `1` |
| Year | `2024-12-31` | `2025-01-01` | `1` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- The order matters: put the earlier date in **First Datetime** to get a positive number.
- For **Day**, the time of day counts. To count calendar days, give both dates the same time (for example `00:00:00`).
- A date with a UTC offset is first converted to UTC. An empty date stops the step with an error.
