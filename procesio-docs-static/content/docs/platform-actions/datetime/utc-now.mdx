title: "UTC Now"

description: "Returns the current date and time in UTC."
---

**UTC Now** gives you the current date and time, in UTC, down to fractions of a second. Use it to timestamp an event, to measure how long part of a process took (with [Time Difference](/how-to/time-difference)), or as the base for a deadline.

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **UTC Now** (or type *UTC Now* in the search box).

![The UTC Now action properties: the UTC Now output](/images/docs/actions/datetime-utc-now/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **UTC Now** | Output | Date and time | Yes | Map a Date and time variable here. It receives the current UTC date and time. |

The action has no inputs.

## Example [#example]
A run on 10 November 2026 at 13:05 UTC returned **UTC Now** `2026-11-10T13:05:31.175287Z`. The `Z` at the end means UTC.

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- The value is always UTC, whatever time zone you or your users are in. To show it in local time, use [Timezone Converter](/how-to/timezone-converter) (from **Etc/UTC** or another UTC zone to your zone).
- Each run of the action reads the clock again. If you need the same timestamp in several places, run **UTC Now** once and reuse its variable.
