title: "Is Weekday"

description: "Checks whether a date falls on a working day, Monday to Friday."
---

**Is Weekday** tells you whether a date is between Monday and Friday. Use it to run a step only on working days, for example to send invoices or to check that a due date does not fall on a weekend.

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Is Weekday** (or type *Is Weekday* in the search box).

![The Is Weekday action properties: Date and Is it weekday?](/images/docs/actions/datetime-is-weekday/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Date** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The date to check. |
| **Is it weekday?** | Output | Boolean (true/false) | Yes | `true` for Monday to Friday, `false` for Saturday and Sunday. |

## Example [#example]
| Date | Is it weekday? |
| --- | --- |
| `2026-11-10` (Tuesday) | `true` |
| `2026-11-07` (Saturday) | `false` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- Monday to Friday always count as weekdays. Public holidays are not taken into account.
- An empty **Date** stops the step with an error.
- **Time zone:** a date that carries a UTC offset (for example `2024-03-01T00:30:00+02:00`) is first converted to UTC, and the result is read from the UTC value. A date without an offset, or ending in `Z`, is used as it is.
