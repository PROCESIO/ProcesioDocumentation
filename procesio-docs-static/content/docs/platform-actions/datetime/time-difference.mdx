---
title: "Time Difference"
icon: "📕"
description: "Counts the hours, minutes or seconds between two dates and times."
---

**Time Difference** tells you how far apart two moments are, in hours, minutes or seconds. Use it to measure how long a step took, to check a response time against an SLA, or to see whether a token has expired. For days and longer, use [Date Difference](/how-to/date-difference).

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **DateTime** and pick **Time Difference** (or type *Time Difference* in the search box).

![The Time Difference action properties: Unit of Time, First Datetime, Second Datetime and Difference](/images/docs/actions/datetime-time-difference/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unit of Time** | Input | Option list | Yes | **Second**, **Minute** or **Hour**. |
| **First Datetime** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The start moment. |
| **Second Datetime** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The end moment. |
| **Difference** | Output | Integer | Yes | **Second Datetime** minus **First Datetime**, in whole units. Negative when the second moment is earlier. |

## Example [#example]
| Unit of Time | First Datetime | Second Datetime | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Second | `2024-01-01T13:25:10` | `2024-01-01T13:26:00` | `50` |
| Minute | `2024-01-01T13:25:10` | `2024-01-01T14:25:09` | `59` (one second short of an hour) |
| Hour | `2024-01-01T22:00:00` | `2024-01-03T01:30:00` | `27` |
| Hour | `2024-01-01T22:00:00` | `2024-01-01T19:30:00` | `-2` |
| Hour | `2024-01-01T12:00:00Z` | `2024-01-01T12:00:00+02:00` | `-2` |
| Minute | `13:25:10` | `14:00:00` | `34` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
- The full date counts, not only the time: 22:00 on one day to 01:30 two days later is 27 hours.
- Partial units are dropped, never rounded up: 59 minutes and 59 seconds is `59` minutes.
- If you give only a time (`13:25:10`), both values are taken on the same day.
- Offsets are respected: both values are converted to UTC before they are compared, so 12:00 UTC and 12:00 at +02:00 are 2 hours apart.

<Callout type="warn">
The result must fit in a whole number of about ±2.1 billion. In **Second**, that is about 68 years. A longer span does not stop the step; it returns a wrong number (1950 to 2030 in seconds returned `-1770359296`). Use **Minute** or **Hour**, or [Date Difference](/how-to/date-difference), for long periods.
</Callout>
