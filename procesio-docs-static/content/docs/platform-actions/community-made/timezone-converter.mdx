**Timezone Converter** takes a date and time as it reads on the clock in one time zone and gives you the same moment on the clock in another time zone. Daylight saving time is applied for both zones on that date. Use it to show a UTC time to users in their local time, or to turn a local appointment time into UTC before you store it.

## Add the action [#add-the-action]
In the Process Designer toolbox, open **Community-Made** and pick **Timezone Converter** (or type *Timezone Converter* in the search box).

![The Timezone Converter action properties: Datetime, From, To and Converted Datetime](/images/docs/actions/community-made-timezone-converter/panel.png)

## Inputs and outputs [#inputs-and-outputs]
| Field | Direction | Type | Required | What to put / what you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Datetime** | Input | Date and time | Yes | The clock time to convert, **without** an offset, for example `2026-01-15T09:00:00`. |
| **From** | Input | Option list | Yes | The time zone that clock time is in, for example *(UTC+02:00) Eastern European Time (Bucharest)* or *(UTC) Coordinated Universal Time*. |
| **To** | Input | Option list | Yes | The time zone you want the clock time in. |
| **Converted Datetime** | Output | Date and time | Yes | The same moment, as the clock reads in **To**. |

The time zone names show the **standard** (winter) offset. The real offset on your date, summer time included, is applied automatically.

## Example [#example]
| Datetime | From | To | Converted Datetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2026-01-15T09:00:00` | Bucharest | New York | `2026-01-15T02:00:00` |
| `2026-07-15T09:00:00` | Bucharest | UTC | `2026-07-15T06:00:00Z` (summer time, UTC+3) |
| `2026-11-10T22:00:00` | UTC | Kolkata | `2026-11-11T03:30:00` |

## Good to know [#good-to-know]
<Callout type="warn">
Give **Datetime** without an offset and without `Z`. The **From** zone decides how it is read:
- `2026-11-10T22:00:00Z` from Bucharest to UTC gives `20:00Z`: the `Z` is ignored and 22:00 is read as Bucharest time.
- `2026-11-10T22:00:00+02:00` from Bucharest to UTC gives `18:00Z`: the value is first moved to UTC (20:00), and then read again as Bucharest time.

If your value comes from [UTC Now](/how-to/utc-now), choose **UTC** in **From**.
</Callout>

- When **To** is UTC, the result ends in `Z`. For other zones the result has no offset.
- A clock time that does not exist in **From**, because the clocks jump forward for summer time (for example `2026-03-29T03:30:00` in Bucharest), stops the step with *The supplied DateTime represents an invalid time.*
- An empty **Datetime** stops the step with `Input Error: Please provide a non-empty DateTime !`.
