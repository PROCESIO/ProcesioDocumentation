**For:** importers, manufacturers, retailers and agri-food companies that can only report once their suppliers have sent them data.

| Regulation | Status in November 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| CBAM | Definitive period since 1 January 2026. Importers above the 50-tonne annual threshold need authorised declarant status and must report the emissions embedded in their imports. The first annual declaration, for 2026 imports, is due by 30 September 2027. |
| EUDR | Due diligence that cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood are deforestation-free. After the December 2025 revision, it applies from 30 December 2026, and from 30 June 2027 for micro and small operators. |
| CSRD | Sustainability reporting. The 2025 Omnibus simplification postponed the later waves by two years and narrowed the scope to companies with more than 1,000 employees and over €450 million net turnover. |

<Callout type="warn">
All three regulations have been amended recently. Confirm the scope and dates that apply to your company with the official source or your advisers.
</Callout>

## The problem today

Requests go out by email with a spreadsheet attached. Answers come back in a dozen formats, some complete, some half-filled, some never. Someone keeps a list of who has answered and chases the rest. When the report is due, the evidence is spread across inboxes and folders.

## The step ordinary tools miss

It's a collection campaign, with hundreds of counterparties, reminders, partial answers, validation, and an audit trail that has to hold up years later. The data is also commercially sensitive, so where it is stored matters as much as how it is collected.

## How it works in PROCESIO

1. **Each supplier gets its own request.** A [form](/forms-tasks-designer) is assigned to the supplier's contact as a task, with only the questions that apply to them.
2. **Answers are checked on entry.** Required fields, units and ranges are validated in the form. Spreadsheets the supplier uploads are read with [Get File Data](/how-to/get-file-data) and checked line by line.
3. **The campaign tracks itself.** A [Data Store](/data-store) records who has answered, who hasn't, and what is still missing. A [Schedule](/schedules) sends reminders and escalates to your buyer after the deadline.
4. **The output is a document, signed off.** [Generate Document](/how-to/generate-document) assembles the declaration or the summary, and an [Approval](/approval) records who signed it off and when.
5. **The data stays where it must.** On PROCESIO cloud it stays in the EU and isn't transferred outside the European Economic Area. Self-hosted, it never leaves your servers.

## What you get

- One campaign view instead of a chase list in a spreadsheet.
- Supplier answers validated as they arrive, so problems surface long before the report is due.
- Evidence with dates, sources and approvals, kept in one place for as long as you need it.
